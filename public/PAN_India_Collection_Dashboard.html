Finance Methodology & Accounting Principles

1. Balance Equation

Outstanding Amount = Max(0, Invoice Amount − Total Collection Amount)

2. Collection Efficiency %

Efficiency % = (Total Collection Amount / Total Invoiced Billing) × 100

3. Ageing Buckets

Calculated as Current Date minus Due Date (for unpaid/partially paid invoices): Not Due, 1–30 Days, 31–60 Days, 61–90 Days, Above 90 Days.
